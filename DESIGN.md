## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined intersection between high-end editorial aesthetics and intimate, familial warmth. Crafted for an upscale hair salon atelier, the interface moves away from sterile cosmetic tropes in favor of tactile elegance, deliberate whitespace, and quiet sophistication.

### Personality & Values
- **Intimate Warmth:** An inviting atmosphere that balances professional mastery with familial approachability.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-fashion publication sensibilities defined by deliberate serif typography, airy framing, and curated imagery.
- **Understated Craft:** Restrained ornamentation where micro-borders, subdued hues, and deliberate typography do the storytelling.

### Visual Language
The aesthetic bridges **Soft Luxury Minimalism** and **Editorial Layout**. Structure relies on natural breathing room, delicate tonal transitions, and whispering contrast. Surfaces echo natural paper and soft skincare tones, avoiding synthetic cool greys or harsh jet blacks.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from vintage French wine notes, cosmetic balms, and tactile unbleached paper.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#8D3158` - Bordeaux Prune):** Anchors high-priority actions, statement typographic moments, and key interactive outlines. Evokes depth, craft, and authority.
- **Secondary (`#D58FA8` - Rose Poudré):** Acts as a soft complement for tags, secondary visual accents, active indicator states, and subtle highlight backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#F2D8E1` - Voile Rosé):** Reserved for subtle container tints, selected state backdrops, and gentle hover washes.
- **Canvas / Background (`#FAF0ED` - Blanc Rosé):** A warm, tinted ambient foundation that replaces clinical whites to create an enveloping, calm experience.
- **Surface & Cards (`#FFFFFF` & `#FDF8F6`):** Pure white elevates elevated service cards, while soft porcelain beige provides delicate separation without visual breakage.
- **Text & Body (`#302D30` - Anthracite Doux):** A deeply saturated, warm off-black that retains optimal legibility without the harshness of pure black.
- **Subtle Stroke (`rgba(141, 49, 88, 0.12)`):** Delimits cards, table dividers, and subtle component borders with fine couture precision.

## Typography

The typographic pairing contrasts classical Parisian editorial romance with clean, modernist discipline.

### Type Pairing Guidance
- **Headlines & Editorial Callouts (EB Garamond):** Used exclusively for high-emotion moments: hero statements, service titles, narrative introductions, and price signatures. Always set with generous line heights and tight tracking on large scales to retain lyricism.
- **Body & Functional UI (DM Sans):** Governs operational readouts, service descriptions, pricing tables, booking flows, and microcopy. Its geometric purity and neutral baseline keep dense scheduling details clear and legible.
- **Labels & Micro-Accents:** Uppercase styles in DM Sans utilize expanded tracking (`0.08em`) to convey catalog sophistication and structure without heavy weights.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an open, fluid 12-column grid. Generous white space serves as a deliberate luxury marker, offering visual pauses between treatments, pricing tiers, and salon philosophy.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, `4rem` outer margin, `1.5rem` gutter. High-impact lookbook sections can span full width with inner inset margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `2rem` outer margin, `1.25rem` gutter. Side-by-side service cards collapse into single/dual-column balanced formats.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, `1.25rem` outer margin, `1rem` gutter. Imagery stacks gracefully above descriptive and transactional booking modules.

### Rhythm & Vertical Cadence
Editorial rhythm depends on breathing space (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) between distinct narrative blocks. Related content groupings (such as a service title, description, and pricing breakdown) are held closely together using `space-xs` through `space-md`.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids high-contrast shadows or plastic blurs. Depth is achieved via soft tonal layering, hairline border definitions, and subtle, ambient warmth.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** `#FAF0ED` as the overall canvas foundation.
- **Level 1 (Subsurface / Inset):** `#FDF8F6` panels resting directly on the canvas, separated only by `1px solid rgba(141, 49, 88, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Cards & Service Modules):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces featuring a warm, diffuse ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 30px -4px rgba(141, 49, 88, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(48, 45, 48, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Elements & Booking Bar):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with refined definition: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -8px rgba(141, 49, 88, 0.1), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(141, 49, 88, 0.06)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Drawers):** Backdrop dimming uses `#302D30` with `25%` opacity, paired with an ambient card blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).

## Shapes

The geometry balances soft, organic curves with clean architectural lines.

### Radii Application
- **Default (`rounded-md` / 0.5rem):** Standard for input fields, service detail cards, image wraps, and calendar pickers.
- **Pronounced (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem):** Featured hero banners, photographic showcase cards, and dialogue drawers.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full`):** Reserved strictly for primary call-to-action buttons, quick filter chips, and boutique category badges.
- **Editorial Imagery:** Imagery may combine curved corners on top corners while maintaining straight edges on the bottom to evoke curated gallery styling.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Prendre Rendez-vous):** Full pill (`rounded-full`) geometry, `#8D3158` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Micro-border of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. On hover, color transitions smoothly to a deepened plum (`#742748`) with subtle lift.
- **Secondary Action (Découvrir le salon):** `#FAF0ED` fill with `1px solid rgba(141, 49, 88, 0.25)` border and `#8D3158` text. On hover, background shifts to `#F2D8E1` with increased border opacity.
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** DM Sans Medium, tracked, featuring a hairline underline anchored 4px below text with a smooth right-to-left hover expansion.

### Service Cards
- **Structure:** Clean `#FFFFFF` or `#FDF8F6` container framed by an ultra-thin border (`1px solid rgba(141, 49, 88, 0.1)`). 
- **Content Hierarchy:** High-definition photography with a `4:5` portrait aspect ratio at the top, followed by treatment title in EB Garamond (`headline-sm`), duration and description in DM Sans (`body-sm`), and a discreet price tag right-aligned in `#8D3158`.
- **Hover Behavior:** Micro-zoom on photography (`scale: 1.02` with 500ms ease) accompanied by a slight border warm shift.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Base Style:** Full pill form, height of 32px, `space-md` horizontal padding.
- **Inactive State:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(141, 49, 88, 0.15)`, text in `#302D30`.
- **Active State:** `#8D3158` background with `#FFFFFF` text, zero border offset.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Base Style:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(141, 49, 88, 0.2)`, `rounded-md`, 48px height for effortless mobile touch targets.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#8D3158`, reinforced with a soft halo `0 0 0 3px rgba(213, 143, 168, 0.25)`. Outline is disabled.
- **Labels:** Floating or top-aligned `label-md` in `#302D30` with `70%` opacity.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular, `1px solid rgba(141, 49, 88, 0.4)`. When active, displays a solid `#8D3158` central dot with an inner white margin.
- **Checkboxes:** Soft square (`rounded-sm`), transitions to `#8D3158` background with a delicate `#FFFFFF` checkmark when selected.

### Salon-Specific Specialty Components
- **Ritual Treatment Timeline:** A vertical dotted hairline in `#D58FA8` with Bordeaux circular milestones, mapping multi-step balayage, scalp treatment, and styling sessions.
- **Artisan Stylist Badge:** Circular portrait avatar framed in a `2px` border with `#F2D8E1` offset, accompanied by name in `headline-sm` and salon expertise tags.
- **Editorial Price List Item:** Borderless list row featuring service name, dotted baseline leader (`border-bottom: 1px dotted rgba(141, 49, 88, 0.25)`), and final price set in EB Garamond.
